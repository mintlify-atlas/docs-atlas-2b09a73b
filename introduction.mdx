ClusterFuzz is a scalable fuzzing infrastructure built by Google to find security and stability issues in software automatically. Google uses it to fuzz all Google products and as the fuzzing backend for [OSS-Fuzz](https://github.com/google/oss-fuzz), where it has helped identify over 27,000 bugs in Google products and more than 36,000 bugs across hundreds of open source projects. ClusterFuzz handles the entire fuzzing lifecycle — from running fuzz targets across large bot fleets to deduplicating crashes, filing bugs, and verifying fixes — without manual intervention.

## Key features

ClusterFuzz is designed to integrate fuzzing seamlessly into a software project's development process. Its core capabilities include:

- **Highly scalable**: runs on clusters of any size. OSS-Fuzz operates ClusterFuzz across up to 100,000 VMs.
- **Accurate crash deduplication**: automatically groups duplicate crashes so teams see each unique bug once.
- **Automatic bug filing and triage**: integrates with issue trackers such as [Monorail](https://opensource.google.com/projects/monorail) and [Jira](https://www.atlassian.com/software/jira) to file, update, and close bugs automatically.
- **Multiple fuzzing engines**: supports [libFuzzer](http://llvm.org/docs/LibFuzzer.html), [AFL++](https://github.com/AFLplusplus/AFLplusplus), and [Honggfuzz](https://github.com/google/honggfuzz) with ensemble fuzzing and per-engine strategies for optimal coverage.
- **Blackbox fuzzing**: supports large, non-deterministic targets that cannot be instrumented directly.
- **Testcase minimization**: automatically reduces crashing inputs to their smallest reproducible form.
- **Regression bisection**: locates the exact commit that introduced a bug.
- **Code coverage and statistics**: tracks fuzzer performance and crash rates over time.
- **Web UI**: provides a management interface for viewing crashes, jobs, fuzzers, and corpora.
- **Authentication**: integrates with various identity providers via [Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/docs/auth).

## How ClusterFuzz fits into your workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Deploy your bot fleet">
    ClusterFuzz bots continuously pull fuzzing tasks from a central server. You can run a single local bot for development or scale to thousands of VMs in production.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload instrumented builds">
    Compile your code with sanitizer instrumentation (ASan, MSan, UBSan) and upload a build archive to Google Cloud Storage. ClusterFuzz picks up new builds automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run fuzz targets">
    Bots execute your fuzz targets using the configured engines, managing corpora and applying mutation strategies to maximize coverage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Triage and fix">
    When a crash is found, ClusterFuzz deduplicates it, files a bug in your issue tracker, identifies the regression range via bisection, and verifies the fix once the bug is closed.
  </Step>
</Steps>
